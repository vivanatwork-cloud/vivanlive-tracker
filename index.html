📅 Day-of-Week Edge Tuesday problem
⚡ The Uncomfortable Truth

My top 5 days made 72% of all profit.

Top 10 days = 115% of net profit — meaning everything after that basically cancelled out. The result wasn't built on grinding consistency. It was built on catching a handful of big moves and not blowing up in between. That's fragile, and it's the thing I'm working on next.

🧠What The Data Taught Me
🩸

My losses were bigger than my wins

Average win day: +$2,161. Average loss day: −$2,541. I won more often (65%) but lost harder when I lost. A profit factor of 1.60 is positive but thin — a hard daily stop-loss would've barely touched my green days while cutting the worst reds in half.

📉

May nearly ended the run

−$19,459 that month. A 38% win rate and a cluster of big red days within a week. The equity curve shows me revenge-trading — loss, loss, bigger loss — sizing up into a losing streak instead of stepping away. I gave back 74% of my entire profit at the worst point.

🔴

Tuesday is my kryptonite

I lost −$19,883 on Tuesdays alone — almost exactly my entire May loss. Meanwhile Sundays and Mondays I win 81%+ of the time. Too consistent across 14 Tuesdays to be noise.

🚀

Then June happened — and I became a different trader

After the May blowup: June 92% win days, July 70%, August +$27K (my best month). Something clicked — I got more selective and stopped forcing trades. That behavioral shift, not any single setup, is the real edge. Bottling whatever changed on June 4th is the whole game now.
